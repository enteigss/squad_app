1. 📊 Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account and use LinkUp BU, we collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, display name
  • Profile Information: Bio, age, location, gender, interests
  • Profile Photos: Images you upload to your profile
  • Communications: Messages you send in chats and hangouts
  • Feedback: Reviews, ratings, and feedback you provide

Information We Collect Automatically

When you use our app, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs, app performance metrics
  • Online Status: When you're active in the app
📱 Important: We do NOT collect your device contacts. Contact permissions have been explicitly removed from our app.

8. 👶 Children's Privacy

LinkUp BU is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will:

  • Delete the information immediately
  • Terminate the associated account
  • Not use the information for any purpose

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at jordan@linkupbu.com.
