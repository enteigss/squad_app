Account & Data Deletion Policy

🗑️ How to Delete Your Account

You have the right to request deletion of your LinkUp BU account and all associated personal data at any time. We make this process simple and straightforward.

1
Send us an email
Email us from the same email address associated with your LinkUp BU account
2
Include "Delete My Account" in the subject line
This helps us quickly identify and prioritize your request
3
We'll process your request
Your account and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
4
Receive confirmation
We'll send you an email confirmation once deletion is complete

Contact us at:

jordan@linkupbu.com

Make sure to email from your account's registered email address

📊 What Data Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently remove the following data from our systems:

Data Type Action Timeframe
Profile Information Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Photos & Images Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Messages & Chat History Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Hangout Participation Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
App Usage Analytics Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Account Authentication Permanently Deleted Within 30 days

📝 Data That May Be Retained

For legal, security, and operational reasons, we may retain certain non-personal information:

  • Anonymized usage statistics (no personal identifiers)
  • Records required for legal compliance (fraud prevention, etc.)
  • Technical logs necessary for app security (anonymized)
⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will need to create a new account if you wish to use LinkUp BU again in the future.

⏱️ Processing Timeline

  • Request Acknowledgment: Within 3 business days
  • Identity Verification: 1-2 business days
  • Data Deletion: Within 30 days of verified request
  • Confirmation Email: Sent upon completion
