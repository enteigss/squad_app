🚨 Our Commitment to Child Safety

LinkUp BU maintains a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation. We are committed to creating a safe environment for all users and take immediate action against any content or behavior that endangers children.

This policy outlines our comprehensive approach to protecting children on our platform, including our detection systems, reporting mechanisms, and enforcement actions.

1. 🚫 Prohibited Content and Conduct

Strictly Prohibited Activities

The following activities are absolutely prohibited on LinkUp BU and will result in immediate account termination and reporting to authorities:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any content that sexualizes, exploits, or abuses children
  • Grooming: Attempts to establish inappropriate relationships with minors
  • Sexual Solicitation: Requesting sexual content or encounters from minors
  • Sextortion: Threatening to share intimate images or content involving minors
  • Age Misrepresentation: Adults posing as minors or vice versa
  • Predatory Behavior: Using the platform to target, exploit, or harm children
  • Inappropriate Content: Sharing sexual or graphic content accessible to minors
  • Privacy Violations: Sharing personal information of minors without consent

Content Guidelines

All content on LinkUp BU must be appropriate for users aged 13 and older. We prohibit:

  • Sexual or suggestive content involving minors
  • Content that promotes self-harm or dangerous activities to minors
  • Cyberbullying or harassment of minors
  • Content designed to circumvent our safety measures

2. 🎂 Age Requirements and Verification

Minimum Age Requirement

LinkUp BU requires all users to be at least 13 years old. This age requirement is strictly enforced to comply with COPPA and protect younger children from potential risks.

Age Verification Process

  • Account Creation: Users must confirm their age during registration
  • Profile Information: Age verification through profile setup
  • Ongoing Monitoring: Regular reviews of user profiles and behavior
  • Additional Verification: May request additional proof if age is questioned

Under-Age Account Handling

If we discover a user is under 13:

  • The account is immediately suspended
  • All personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • The user is permanently banned from creating new accounts
  • Parents/guardians are notified if contact information is available

3. 🛡️ Safety Measures and Controls

Automated Detection Systems

  • Content Scanning: AI-powered detection of inappropriate content
  • Behavioral Analysis: Monitoring for suspicious user behavior patterns
  • Communication Monitoring: Automated scanning of messages for concerning content
  • Profile Review: Regular automated and manual review of user profiles

Human Moderation

  • 24/7 moderation team trained in child safety
  • Escalation procedures for serious violations
  • Regular training on emerging threats and detection methods
  • Collaboration with child safety experts and organizations

User Safety Features

  • Reporting Tools: Easy-to-use reporting mechanisms for users
  • Blocking and Muting: Tools to prevent unwanted contact
  • Privacy Controls: Settings to limit who can contact users
  • Location Sharing Controls: Restrictions on sharing precise location data

4. 📢 Reporting Mechanisms

🚨 Report Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior that endangers children, report it immediately:

📧 Report via Email

For immediate danger, contact local authorities first: 911 (US)

How to Report

  1. Immediate Action: If a child is in immediate danger, contact local authorities first
  2. Document Evidence: Take screenshots but do not share CSAM content
  3. Contact Us: Email jordan@linkupbu.com with "URGENT: Child Safety Report" in subject
  4. Provide Details: Include username, timestamps, and description of concerning behavior
  5. Follow Up: We will respond within 1 hour for urgent reports

What We Do With Reports

  • Immediate Review: All child safety reports are prioritized
  • Account Action: Immediate suspension of offending accounts
  • Content Removal: Removal of all inappropriate content
  • Authority Notification: Reporting to NCMEC and law enforcement as required
  • Follow-Up: Regular updates to reporters when possible

6. 👮 Law Enforcement Cooperation

Our Legal Obligations

LinkUp BU is committed to cooperating fully with law enforcement agencies to protect children and prosecute offenders.

Reporting Requirements

  • NCMEC Reports: All CSAM content is immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
  • Law Enforcement: Direct cooperation with FBI, local police, and international authorities
  • Evidence Preservation: All evidence is preserved according to legal requirements
  • Court Orders: Full compliance with subpoenas, warrants, and court orders

International Cooperation

  • Cooperation with international child protection agencies
  • Reporting to appropriate authorities in users' jurisdictions
  • Compliance with international child protection laws
  • Participation in global child safety initiatives

7. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Resources for Parents and Guardians

Keeping Your Teen Safe

We encourage parents and guardians to actively discuss online safety with their teenagers and monitor their digital activities.

🗣️ Communication Tips

  • Have open conversations about online interactions
  • Discuss the risks of sharing personal information
  • Encourage reporting of uncomfortable situations

👀 Monitoring Guidelines

  • Regularly check your teen's online activities
  • Be aware of new apps and platforms
  • Look for changes in behavior or mood

🚨 Warning Signs

  • Secretive behavior about online activities
  • Receiving gifts from unknown sources
  • Unusual emotional responses to device restrictions

🔗 External Resources

  • NetSmartz (NCMEC)
  • ConnectSafely.org
  • Common Sense Media

How to Report Concerns About Your Teen

If you have concerns about your teenager's interactions on LinkUp BU:

  1. Contact us immediately at jordan@linkupbu.com
  2. Include "Parental Concern" in the subject line
  3. Provide your teen's username and specific concerns
  4. If you suspect criminal activity, contact local authorities
